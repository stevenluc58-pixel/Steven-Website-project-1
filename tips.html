Tips & Tricks

Simple ways to improve your cooking every day.

Cooking Tips

  • Prep ingredients before cooking
  • Use fresh ingredients when possible
  • Taste food as you cook
  • Keep your workspace clean
  • Use the right pan for each dish
  • Don’t overcrowd the pan

Meal Prep Tips

  • Plan meals for the week
  • Cook in batches
  • Store food in containers
  • Label your meals
  • Use a grocery list
  • Prep ingredients ahead of time

Kitchen Hacks

  • Use a spoon to peel ginger
  • Freeze herbs in oil
  • Use salt to clean pans
  • Microwave lemons for more juice
  • Keep knives sharp
  • Use paper towels to keep veggies fresh
