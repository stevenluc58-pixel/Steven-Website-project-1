Check off each step as you cook. Your progress will be saved.

Garlic Chicken Pasta (30 min)

Cooking Steps

  • Boil a large pot of salted water
  • Add pasta and cook for 8–10 minutes
  • Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces
  • Heat oil in a pan and cook chicken
  • Add garlic and seasoning
  • Drain pasta and mix with chicken
  • Serve warm and enjoy

Cold Pasta Salad (20 min)

Cooking Steps

  • Boil pasta until tender
  • Drain and rinse with cold water
  • Chop vegetables
  • Mix pasta and vegetables
  • Add dressing
  • Toss everything together
  • Chill before serving

Cheesy Beef Quesadilla (20 min)

Cooking Steps

  • Cook ground beef until browned
  • Add seasoning
  • Place tortilla in pan
  • Add cheese and beef
  • Fold tortilla
  • Cook until crispy
  • Slice and serve

Quick Cooking Reminders

  • Follow steps in order
  • Prep ingredients first
  • Don’t rush cooking
  • Use timers
  • Clean as you go
